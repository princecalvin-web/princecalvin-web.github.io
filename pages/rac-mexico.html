01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

A US production platform, expanding to Mexico.

The application was already in production in the United States as part of the Rent-A-Center ecosystem. A requirement emerged to build a Mexico regional implementation with appropriate business workflows and customer journeys adapted for the Mexican region.

This was not a greenfield build — it was a regional adaptation of a live production platform, requiring careful gap analysis, workflow mapping and close client collaboration.

02 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

The US platform couldn't serve Mexico as-is.

The existing US platform carried US-specific business workflows, customer journeys and operational processes. Deploying it directly into the Mexican market without adaptation would have resulted in misaligned customer experiences and non-functional business workflows.

The core challenge was identifying which elements needed to be adapted, documenting those gaps clearly, and translating them into development-ready requirements — while maintaining close alignment with international stakeholders.

03 / MY ROLE

Client-facing Business & Quality Analyst.

I served as the primary Business Analyst on this engagement — working directly with international Product Owners and business stakeholders to gather, define and validate requirements. My responsibility extended from initial requirement elicitation through to UAT and production validation.
