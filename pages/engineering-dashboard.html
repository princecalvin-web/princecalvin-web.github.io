01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

Leadership needed engineering visibility.

Practice Heads and Project Managers needed a structured, data-driven view of engineering performance across teams. The data already existed — in JIRA — but there was no consolidated platform to surface and analyse it in a meaningful way.

The core purpose of this dashboard is to track employee performance, team contribution, project contribution, resource allocation and AI adoption.

02 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

No unified view of engineering performance.

Without a centralised analytics platform, leadership had limited visibility into team-level and individual-level performance. Identifying delivery bottlenecks, resource allocation gaps and AI adoption patterns required manual effort and was inconsistent.

03 / MY ROLE

Business Analyst — requirement definition to delivery.

I was responsible for defining the requirements, rubrics and business logic that drove the dashboard's analytical model. This included defining the Engineering Score calculation model and translating leadership needs into functional specifications.

04 / ENGINEERING SCORE MODEL

Velocity + Quality = Engineering Score.

The dashboard uses a structured scoring model to produce a single Engineering Score per engineer, combining two core dimensions:
