01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

Story estimation — slow, inconsistent, manual.

Story-point estimation is a fundamental part of Agile delivery. Without accurate estimates, sprint planning suffers, delivery predictability drops and capacity planning becomes unreliable.

This Business Efficient Automation use case was designed to support story-point estimation using a predefined domain Knowledge Base, LLM and Retrieval-Augmented Generation — bringing consistency and context-awareness to the estimation process.

02 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

Estimation relied on individual judgement.

Traditional story estimation was heavily dependent on the assignee's familiarity with the domain and past experience. This created inconsistency across teams and sprints, and made capacity planning harder to standardise.

The challenge was to create an AI-assisted workflow that could use historical domain knowledge to support more consistent, rubric-based estimates — while still feeding the result directly into JIRA.

03 / MY ROLE

Business Analyst — workflow, rules and validation.

My contribution was centred around business analysis, requirement definition, business rule design and validation. I did not personally engineer the LLM or RAG architecture — that was handled by the engineering team. My role was to define what the workflow should do, how it should behave, and to validate that the delivered solution met the defined business requirements.
