01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

JIRA workspaces drift without governance.

As teams scale, JIRA workspaces accumulate hygiene issues — unconfigured fields, missing assignees, stale tickets, incomplete statuses. Without a structured monitoring tool, Project Managers had no efficient way to identify and action these deviations.

The business purpose of this dashboard is to help Project Management teams keep JIRA workspaces clean, consistent and compliant with defined PM checklist criteria.

02 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

No visibility into JIRA workspace health.

Project Managers lacked a central, structured view of JIRA workspace compliance. Identifying deviations from PM checklists required manual, time-consuming investigation. Escalation decisions were difficult to make without consolidated data.

03 / MY ROLE

Business Analyst — requirements to delivery support.

I was responsible for requirement clarification, business analysis, functional validation and testing. I supported the delivery team in translating PM checklist criteria into functional dashboard requirements, and validated the solution against defined business rules.
